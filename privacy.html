DUBLDOWN Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

DUBLDOWN ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Your name, phone number, and profile photo when you create an account.
  • Contacts: With your permission, we access your contacts to help you find and add friends on DUBLDOWN.
  • Status Updates: The availability statuses and plans you post in the app.
  • Usage Data: Basic analytics about how you use the app to help us improve it.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core functionality of showing your status to friends
  • To help you find and connect with friends
  • To send you notifications about friend activity
  • To improve the app experience

Information Sharing

Your status and profile are only visible to friends you've added. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption and security practices.

Your Choices

  • You can update or delete your account at any time in the app settings
  • You can control who sees your status by managing your friends list
  • You can opt out of notifications in your device settings

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@dubldown.app

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify you of any significant changes through the app.
