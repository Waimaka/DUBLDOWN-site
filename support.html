How do I add friends?

Go to the Friends tab and search your contacts. You can also share your profile link directly with friends.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This will permanently remove all your data.

Why can't my friend see my status?

Make sure you've both added each other as friends. Statuses are only visible to mutual friends.

How do I change my notification settings?

Go to Settings > Notifications, or manage notifications in your device's Settings app.
